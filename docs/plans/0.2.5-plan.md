## 0. Summary

- **Mission:** one output per device that a technician copies once and pastes once, with nothing to select, uncomment or judge; above it, in plain words, what is irregular on the device against the standard and what the paste does about each item; below it, the one line to type after the check. The output shows irregularities and gives the commands that establish consistency; tacctl writes nothing to a device.
- **Shape:** `device config show` stays the fresh-device walkthrough; `device config plan` is the per-device output (D94): the **irregularity report** (D110), then **one delimited paste region** (D108) that is **safe when pasted whole** because it ends at the confirmed commit on Junos and at `end` on IOS, so the paste itself can make no permanent change (D109); extras are reported and, with `--converge`, removed by a second, separately delimited cleanup region pasted only after the first is verified (D111); the wrong-device, pager and paste-speed risks are handled as far as they can be (D112); secrets by the 0.2.4 rule (D98); the planner of the deferred plan is the engine (D102); Cisco on the IOS 12.4 findings (D100, D106, D107); WTI waits for its own release (D101); tests without a device apply (D103); tiers, audit, settings and `rollback 0.2.4` (D104, D105).
- **Size:** 9 packages, ≈ 7-9 sessions (D108-D112 change the emitters' layout, not their engine; `--wrapper replace` is dropped and `--converge` added, about even). Cut order: `--converge`, then the Cisco emitter, then `--json`, then `--out-dir` (§10).

## 1. Goals and non-goals

### Goals

1. **`device config plan`** (D94): per device, from the last pull (`--pull` first), the report and the region, for an engineer (own scopes) and a superuser alike.
2. **One paste region, nothing to select** (D108); **safe when pasted whole** (D109); **the report first, in plain words** (D110); **consistency, extras included, by `--converge`** (D111); **tech-proofing** (D112).
3. **Lock-out safety by construction** (D97, D106): order, never an empty authentication order, the confirmed commit as the only commit, the lo0 application and the `access-class` binding never emitted, refusals where a region could cut the person off.
4. **Secrets** (D98): pasted when the device lacks them, never printed when present, `--secrets`; `--no-secrets` makes the line non-loadable.
5. **Formats** (D99): the report and region on the terminal, `--plain` (the region only), `--json`, `--section`, a selection with a cover page and one file per device.
6. **Cisco** (D100, D106, D107): the same delta in IOS lines on the live IOS 12.4 and 15.6 findings; the region is `configure terminal` … `end` on every IOS; no revert timer (it is no safety net when the paste cuts the tech's own session [S: 15.6]).
7. **Verified without a device apply** (D103): planner goldens, the whole-paste invariant test, an in-memory property test, bats, private loads on the lab switch, the restored runs on the lab router.
8. **`tacctl rollback 0.2.4`** (D105).

### Non-goals (0.2.5)

- Writing to a device from tacctl: no apply, no enable-netconf, no `devapply` transaction (all in `apply-deferred.md`).
- `--wrapper replace` (the timed `configure replace` path of the earlier D100): dropped from 0.2.5 (D109: it needs a file on the device and prompts that swallow a paste; it cannot be one region).
- WTI blocks (D101: 0.2.6, with the WTI read).
- Emitting `breakglass`, `netconf`, the lo0 application, the `access-class` binding, `system root-authentication`, `system services ssh`, `groups`, `apply-groups`, `username`, `enable secret`, `line vty`/`line con` headers: never, whatever flag is given.
- Removing extras without `--converge` (D111); a rollback verb (the back-out is given outside the region); per-device source address (a kept line).
